FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

How is Kestrel different from an ATS?+

An ATS tracks candidates. Kestrel evaluates them. We sit on top of your existing workflow and add AI screening, role-specific assessments, and structured interviews — so you get a ranked shortlist, not just a database of applicants.

Does the AI interview replace my human panel?+

No. The AI interview replaces the early rounds that waste your senior team's time — the initial screen, the technical quiz, the intro call. Your panel runs the final stage with evidence already in hand.

Can candidates tell they're talking to AI?+

Yes — and we're transparent about it. Candidates are told upfront. In our data, over 85% say the experience felt fair and thorough. A good process builds trust regardless of who's running it.

How long does setup take for a new role?+

Under 15 minutes. You paste in the JD, pick the job profile, adjust the pipeline stages if needed, and it's live. The first candidates can be scored the same day.

Where do your numbers come from?+

Our stats — ~2 min scoring time, ~91% AI confidence, <48 hr shortlist — come from aggregated data across Kestrel deployments in 2025–2026 on roles with 50+ applicants. The cost figure (₹7.5L–18L per bad hire) is sourced from 2026 India hiring cost benchmarking reports covering mid-level roles in the ₹15–25L CTC band.
